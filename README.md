# The World in One Page - Saturday, 21 November 2026

A one-image summary of the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026, drawn as a
newspaper front page: Russian missiles fall in Poland and NATO calls it a grave
violation; the World Court finds Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya;
the AI sell-off pushes the S&P 500 into correction; Trump, Xi and Putin end their
first three-way summit in Shenzhen; the UK and EU sign food, carbon and youth
deals - plus briefs on Hormuz, Riyadh, a pancreatic-cancer breakthrough and the
Palisades Fire verdict.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to the output directory (`/output`, or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` outside the grader). The image is 1024x1024 PNG.
